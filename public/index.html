THE FIELD MANUAL

A little light goes a long way.

Your mission

Two explorers enter the same connected, three-level ruin. You have 240 seconds to charge all four altars and stand together at the final exit for two seconds. The clock never pauses.

Follow the lights

  1. A1 — The First Light. Charge it to unlock level two and your first skill.
  2. A2 + B2. Return to level one for A2 and explore level two for B2. Charge both to unlock level three and a second skill.
  3. C3 — The Last Light. Entering level three wakes the destroyer below. Charge C3, choose your last skill and leave together.

Win together. Fall together.

You lose if both players go down, time runs out, or the world collapses before C3 is full. Once C3 is full, the exit remains, but you still need to reach it before time runs out.

Your equipment

Armor takes damage first and regenerates after five seconds without a hit. Health does not regenerate. Hold E beside a downed partner for two seconds to revive them.

Hidden enemies show a red attack warning. Dodge, shoot them, then press E to collect their stones. An unfinished altar loses energy; enemies begin attacking altars in stage two.

Each stone adds 25 altar energy, or restores 40 mana with R. Your pack holds eight. Filled altars stay filled.
